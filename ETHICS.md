# Ethics & Legal Stance

This project operates under a strict ethical framework. Offensive security capability exists **only to make systems more secure**.

---

## Rules

1. **Authorized targets only.** The system is used against systems the operator owns, is contracted to assess, or has explicit written authorization to test.
2. **No destructive action without human approval.** Every high-impact operation is gated behind a human-in-the-loop checkpoint.
3. **No data exfiltration beyond scope.** Findings are documented for the assessment — not harvested.
4. **Responsible disclosure.** Vulnerabilities found are reported through proper channels, never weaponized publicly.
5. **Compliance with law.** The system respects all applicable computer fraud and access laws in the operator's jurisdiction.

---

## Why This Matters

Security research without ethics is just vandalism. This system is built to **amplify a defender's reach**, not to lower the bar for attacks.

The human-in-the-loop checkpoint is not a feature — it's a **moral requirement**. The AI proposes; the human bears responsibility for approving.

---

## Reporting Concerns

If you believe this project or its operator is being misused, contact the maintainer through the project's public channels. Misuse will not be tolerated.
